JOURNEY

PROTOTYPE RULE

Reach the ruined tower alive. Terrain penalties now take effect the instant you enter that terrain during a move: open ground costs 1 movement, forest costs 2, and mountain costs 3. A fresh roll always allows at least one legal step even if the terrain would normally cost more than the roll remaining. Attempting to cross the river away from a bridge pauses the journey for a temporary crossing roll; its real consequences have not been designed yet.
