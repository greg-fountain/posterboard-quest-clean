Obstacle terrain is denser, but every mountain and forest mass has an open way around it. Open ground has no modifier. Forest gives −1 movement; mountains give −2. Every fresh roll always allows at least 1 move. Entering a new terrain type partway through a move applies its modifier immediately.

PROTOTYPE RULE

Reach the ruined tower alive. Roll d6 for movement. If the turn begins in forest, subtract 1; if it begins in mountains, subtract 2; the result can never be lower than 1. If you enter forest or mountains partway through a move, apply that terrain modifier immediately to the movement you have left. The modifier applies once when that terrain becomes active, not once per square.
